Pursuit Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

Pursuit ("the game", "the app") is a game published by LyfEscapes LLC under the studio name t0ilet b0wl gaMez ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and what it does not.

Summary

Pursuit does not require an account and does not ask for your name or email. The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID to show and measure ads. We do not sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (levels cleared, stars, in-game currency, equipped suits, settings and purchases you have made) in local storage on your device. This information stays on your device. We do not receive it and cannot see it. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it is removed.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information through the game and we do not run servers that receive data from the app. The advertising service described below collects some information directly, as described in that section.

Purchases

If you buy something in the game, the purchase is handled by Google Play. Google processes your payment and shares with us only the information needed to confirm and deliver your purchase (for example, that a product was bought). We never see your payment card details. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Vibration: used for haptic feedback during play.
  • Billing: used by Google Play to complete in-app purchases.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ad service as described above.

The game does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Advertising

The game shows rewarded ads (for example, to earn a bonus) and occasional interstitial ads between runs, using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and information about how you interact with ads to deliver, personalize and measure ads and to prevent fraud. We do not receive this information. Where required, such as in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings under Privacy > Ads. Learn more in how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. The VIP Pass in-app purchase removes ad breaks.

Third-party services

The game uses Google Play services for in-app purchases and Google AdMob for advertising, both described above.

Children

Pursuit is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Data retention and deletion

Because we do not collect your data, there is nothing for us to delete. To remove the information the game stores on your device, clear the app's data in Android settings or uninstall the app.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
